📌 Key Business Insights
📈 November recorded the highest sales and order volume.  
🇬🇧 The United Kingdom contributed over 81% of total revenue.  
🛍️ "WORLD WAR 2 GLIDERS ASSTD DESIGNS" was the best-selling product.  
👥 A small group of customers generated a significant portion of total orders.  
📅 Tuesday and Thursday consistently achieved strong sales performance.  
⚠️ Data analysis also identified anomalies such as zero sales in October, highlighting the importance of data validation.
